## How Kouchd helps

Use the web app to keep stay dates, agreements, household notes, chores, and contribution records together. Hosts manage the stays they run. Guests see their own agreement and individual financial details.

<Note>
  **Outside payments & shared records:** Kouchd records shared agreements and contributions; it does not collect, hold, transfer, or process money. A saved entry is a shared record, not proof that a payment cleared.
</Note>

<Info>
  **App support vs. stay disagreements:** Email [help@kouchd.com](mailto:help@kouchd.com) for technical app issues or account support. Kouchd does not mediate personal conflicts, house rule disputes, or roommate disagreements.
</Info>

For details about what information is shared, read [Privacy and your stay information](/help/legal-trust/privacy-and-security).
