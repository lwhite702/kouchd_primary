A GENEROUS FREE PLAN

Start organizing a shared stay with Kouchd’s free plan. Guests always use Kouchd free, so everyone can stay informed and take part without being asked to subscribe.

KOUCHD PLUS FOR HOSTS

Hosts can upgrade to Kouchd Plus for expanded tools that make a shared stay easier to manage. Choose a monthly or annual plan, and keep every guest on the free experience.

Kouchd helps hosts, guests, and roommates spend less time sorting out details—and more time feeling at home.

Kouchd records agreements and contributions. It does not collect, hold, transfer, or process payments. Payment arrangements happen directly between the people involved, outside Kouchd.
