## Money and the Ledger

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Kouchd process payments?">
    No. Kouchd records contributions. Money moves directly between host and guest, outside the app. Kouchd never holds or transfers it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a contribution prove a payment arrived?">
    No. A contribution is what someone reported, and a confirmed one means the other person agreed with it. It doesn't show that money cleared.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see what I owe or pay?">
    You and your host. Other guests can't see your agreement, charges, or contributions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone recorded the wrong amount. What now?">
    Choose **Dispute** on the entry and talk it through. Then **Void** the wrong record and add a correct one. See [Managing the Ledger](/help/hosts/ledger-management).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export my records?">
    The app doesn't have an export button right now. Email [help@kouchd.com](mailto:help@kouchd.com) if you need a copy of your stay records. Records from ended stays stay readable under **Past stays**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do guests pay for Kouchd?">
    No. Guests are always free. Plans apply only to hosts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Plus cost?">
    On the website, Plus is $6.99 a month or $69.99 a year. See [Plans and limits](/help/hosts/plans-and-limits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does it say a stay is full?">
    The host's plan has no open guest spot. Ask your host to make room. Only the host sees plan details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Stays and invitations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My invite link doesn't work.">
    Links made in the web app expire after 7 days and work once. Ask your host for a new link, or for the stay's join code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I be in more than one stay?">
    Yes. Use **All stays** on the web, or tap the stay name on iPhone, to switch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when a stay ends?">
    New activity stops and the record is kept. Everyone can still read it under **Past stays**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can chores rotate between people?">
    Not yet. Chores can repeat daily or weekly, and rotation is on the [roadmap](/help/product/roadmap) as something we're exploring.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## iPhone app and support

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there an iPhone app?">
    It's in TestFlight and isn't on the App Store yet. Everything also works at [app.kouchd.com](https://app.kouchd.com).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Kouchd settle a disagreement in my household?">
    No. Kouchd keeps agreements written down, but it doesn't mediate between people. For app problems, [we're happy to help](/help/support/app-support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
