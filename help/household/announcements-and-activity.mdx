## Announcements

Hosts can post a note for everyone, like "Plumber coming Tuesday morning." Add a title and message, and optionally **Pin until** a date to keep it prominent. Announcements appear on **Home**.

## Recent activity

**Home** shows a running list of what has changed in the stay, such as new chores or updated terms. Guests don't see other guests' private money details here.

## Notifications in the app

The web app also shows notifications for things that need you, such as a contribution to confirm or an agreement to review. For email and text, see [Email and text reminders](/help/notifications/notification-preferences).
