---
title: "House check-ins"
description: "Make room for a conversation between a host and a guest, and keep a record of it."
---

A check-in is a small written conversation between a host and one guest. Use it for things like a shared kitchen routine or how the first week went.

## Start a check-in

1. Open **House check-ins** in the web sidebar, or **Check-ins** under **More** on iPhone.
2. Pick a date and a topic, and add notes on what would help to discuss. Hosts also choose the guest.
3. Save it. It shows up in the **Check-in history**.

## Keep it moving

- **Respond:** Either of you can add a response or next step.
- **Set a status:** Mark it for follow-up or **Mark resolved**. You can reopen it later.

Guests see only their own check-ins. Hosts see check-ins for their stay.

<Info>
  This is different from the **first check-in date** you set when creating a stay, which is the arrival date used for a [reminder](/help/notifications/notification-preferences). Kouchd also doesn't step in on disagreements, so a check-in is a record for the two of you.
</Info>
