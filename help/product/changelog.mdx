This changelog highlights user-facing updates and verified product releases. It does not list every internal code or configuration change. The [roadmap](/help/product/roadmap) separates current work from available features.

## November 2026

- **Door and Wi-Fi codes stay protected:** Guests see the door code and Wi-Fi password only after their agreement is accepted, and they stay visible until the stay ends. A guest who leaves and rejoins gets a new agreement, and the codes appear again once it's accepted, by the guest or automatically after the host's waiting window. Hosts always see their own codes.
- **Log a purchase from the shopping list (web):** After checking off a shared item, choose **Log contribution** to open **Add contribution** with the item already filled in. You still enter the amount, and a host with several guests chooses whose Ledger it goes on. Personal items never offer this. The record is a note between you and your household; it doesn't move money.
- **Guest spots at a glance for hosts (web):** The plan button shows how many guest spots you're using, and you'll see a notice when someone tried to join while your spots were full.
- **Steadier sign-in and easier keyboard use:** The sign-in and sign-up buttons stay inactive while your details are being checked, so they can't be pressed twice, and the example stay preview on the home page works with the arrow keys.
- **New comparison guide:** [How Kouchd compares to Splitwise and Flatastic](/help/support/kouchd-vs-splitwise) explains what Kouchd is for and what it doesn't do.

## October 2026

- **Prerelease numbering:** Kouchd is in prerelease (version 0.7) while we prepare for the App Store. October builds that were briefly labeled 1.0 release candidates are now numbered 0.7.0 through 0.7.12. Version 1.0 starts when the App Store submission is approved.
- **Delayed guest agreement acceptance:** Hosts can now set a waiting window (or require manual acceptance) before pending agreements take effect, while guests can review and accept immediately at any time.
- **Liquid Glass & fluid motion:** Refined app motion with gentle, organic transitions and clean glass surfaces that reduce visual noise across the dashboard.
- **Dedicated app support documentation:** Added clear guidance on reaching our support team at `help@kouchd.com` for technical app help, while clarifying that Kouchd does not mediate personal stay conflicts or landlord disputes.
- **Modular iPhone app guides:** Expanded mobile guidance into three dedicated topic guides in the Help Center covering getting started, ledger contributions, and household chores.
- **Loops newsletter signup:** Added an optional stay-tips newsletter for hosts and guests.
- **Clerk profile display names:** Household members now see real profile names synced from account profiles across the dashboard, ledger, and chore boards instead of generic labels.
- **Brand design system and visual refresh:** Implemented the warm Kouchd brand design system across web surfaces, featuring Manrope typography, soft rounded cards, Forest Green anchors, and accessible contrast palettes.
- **Streamlined web app entry:** Enhanced sign-in and direct workspace routing so returning members arrive directly at their active household dashboard.

## September 2026

- **Individual guest agreements:** Hosts can set different dates and contribution terms for each guest. Guests review and accept the agreement that applies to them; household guidelines remain shared.
- **External payment options:** Hosts can share payment details or links through Stay details. Guests pay the host directly outside Kouchd.
- **Payment reminder updates:** Reminder information now reflects the due date, accepted grace period, late-fee terms, and current ledger. Email and optional SMS remain controlled by separate saved preferences.
- **More control over reminders:** Email and SMS are separate preferences. SMS requires a mobile number and an explicit opt-in, with message-rate, frequency, STOP, and HELP details shown before you subscribe.
- **Clearer help for shared stays:** Host and guest guides now explain agreements, household contributions, privacy, and the fact that payments happen outside Kouchd.
- **Notification guidance:** Added steps for changing reminder preferences and opting out of texts.
