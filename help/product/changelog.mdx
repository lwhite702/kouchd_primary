title: Kouchd product changelog
description: Recent improvements to Kouchd and its help center.
---

This changelog highlights user-facing updates. It does not list every internal code or configuration change. The [roadmap](/help/product/roadmap) separates current work from available features.

## October 2026

- **Native iOS app release:** Introduced a native SwiftUI iOS app for iPhone and iPad with feature parity for shared stays, guest invitations, stay agreements, household chores, and outside contribution records. Includes typed navigation, dark and light mode support, and accessibility accommodations.
- **Figma V2.1 web host overview:** Redesigned the authenticated host overview with a warm home base hero panel, a three-metric summary row (Guests, Contributions, Chores), needs-attention alerts, and a real-time household activity timeline.
- **Dedicated workspace domain:** Routed the authenticated stay workspace through `app.kouchd.com` while maintaining public marketing, feature tours, and documentation on `kouchd.com`.
- **Master legal and trust policies:** Added dedicated pages and help documentation for Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Acceptable Use, and Carrier 10DLC SMS compliance.
- **Help Center visual refresh:** Added brand green ambient styling, interactive guidance callouts, and canonical link mapping across onboarding wizards and in-app navigation.

## September 2026

- **Individual guest agreements:** Hosts can set different dates and contribution terms for each guest. Guests review and accept the agreement that applies to them; household guidelines remain shared.
- **External payment options:** Hosts can share payment details or links through Stay details. Guests pay the host directly outside Kouchd.
- **Payment reminder updates:** Reminder information now reflects the due date, accepted grace period, late-fee terms, and current ledger. Email and optional SMS remain controlled by separate saved preferences.
- **More control over reminders:** Email and SMS are separate preferences. SMS requires a mobile number and an explicit opt-in, with message-rate, frequency, STOP, and HELP details shown before you subscribe.
- **Clearer help for shared stays:** Host and guest guides now explain agreements, household contributions, privacy, and the fact that payments happen outside Kouchd.
- **Notification guidance:** Added steps for changing reminder preferences and opting out of texts.
