<Tip>
  We shape Kouchd around the everyday details that help shared stays work well. This roadmap outlines current features and active development without promised dates.
</Tip>

## Available in the current apps

- **Shared stay coordination:** Shared stay details, house guidelines, notes, and chores across web and native iOS.
- **Contribution ledger:** Outside contribution records for payments arranged directly between host and guest, groceries, supplies, and other agreed help.
- **Individual guest agreements:** Hosts tailor stay dates and contribution terms per guest while keeping house guidelines shared. Each guest reviews and accepts their own agreement.
- **Host-shared external payment options:** Hosts share direct handles or links for PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay. Guests pay hosts directly outside Kouchd.
- **Figma V2.1 host overview:** Authenticated host dashboard with a warm home base hero card, 3 summary cards (Guests, Contributions, Chores), needs-attention alerts, and recent household activity.
- **Native iOS app:** Native SwiftUI client for iPhone and iPad with typed route navigation, dark/light modes, and full stay, agreement, chore, and contribution logging parity.
- **Dedicated workspace routing:** Authenticated stays operate through `app.kouchd.com` with marketing and help resources on `kouchd.com`.
- **Payment & agreement reminders:** Milestone reminders reflecting due dates, accepted grace periods, and ledger status, delivered via email and optional opt-in transactional SMS.

## Not available in Kouchd

<Warning>
  **In-app payment processing:** Kouchd does not collect, hold, transfer, or process payments. Hosts and guests arrange payments directly using shared external payment details or other methods they choose.
</Warning>

## In progress

- **Shared household coordination:** Shopping lists with personal or shared items, recurring chores with due reminders and automatic advancement, and structured host/guest check-in records are being added to the web app. These features are in active development and are not yet available in released builds.
- **Flexible payment schedules and recorded overnights:** Daily or weekly contributions, weekday selection for weekly payments, schedule change requests with host approval, a $0 due-balance gate, and host-recorded overnight billing for nonconsecutive dates.
- **Household subscriptions and host tiers:** Server-authoritative Starter, Host Plus, and Household Pro tiers via RevenueCat for household hosts, keeping guests free.
- **Automated agreement reminders:** Progressive reminders for pending agreements delivered via Knock with cancellation upon acceptance.

## Being explored

- **Native Android experience:** Exploration of Android design and native coordination workflows. TestFlight and App Store availability for iOS builds (0.4.0–0.4.2) continue through our release verification pipeline.
