<Tip>
  We shape Kouchd around the everyday details of sharing a home. Kouchd is in **prerelease** (version 0.7) while we prepare for the App Store, so plans can change and we don't promise dates.
</Tip>

## Available now

- **Individual guest agreements:** Each guest has their own dates, rate, schedule, and fees, with shared house rules.
- **Automatic acceptance:** Hosts can set a waiting period for pending agreements, or turn it off.
- **The Ledger:** Charges, contributions, confirming, disputing, and voiding, kept private between each guest and host.
- **Payment options:** Hosts can share how to pay them. Guests pay outside Kouchd.
- **Chores:** A shared board with assignments, due dates, and chores that repeat daily or weekly.
- **Shopping list:** Shared and personal items. Items don't affect balances.
- **House check-ins:** Dated conversations between a host and a guest, with responses and a status.
- **House guide and announcements:** Everyday home details and notes for the whole house.
- **Kouchd Coins:** Optional rewards that guests can redeem as credit.
- **Past stays:** Ended stays stay readable.
- **Reminders:** Email and optional text, with separate settings.
- **Plans:** Free and Plus for hosts. Guests are always free.

## Rolling out

- **Schedule change requests and occupied nights:** Available to some stays as we finish them. See [the guide](/help/hosts/schedules-and-overnights).
- **Plus purchases:** Being rolled out on the web and in the iPhone app.

## What Kouchd does not handle

<Warning>
  **In-app payment processing:** Kouchd does not collect, hold, transfer, or process payments. Hosts and guests arrange payments directly using external payment methods they choose.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Stay disputes & conflict resolution:** Kouchd is a shared coordination and record-keeping tool. Kouchd does not mediate, arbitrate, or handle personal disagreements, house rule conflicts, or living situation disputes between hosts and guests. Household members are responsible for communicating with each other and resolving disagreements directly.
</Warning>

## In progress

- **iPhone app:** In TestFlight today, on its way to the App Store.
- **Reliability and accessibility:** Keyboard focus, screen-reader labels, and tap targets across the app.

## Being explored (Post-1.0 roadmap)

- **Receipt scanning for shared groceries:** Attach a photo of a store receipt to pre-fill a shared grocery or supply contribution entry.
- **iPhone push notifications:** Alerts on your phone alongside email and text.
- **Chore rotation:** Take turns on a repeating chore, with a set order, a way to skip someone who's away, and a record of who was reassigned.
- **Household supplies:** Keep track of what's on hand, such as paper towels or dish soap, and get a nudge to add it to the shopping list when it runs low. Nothing is bought or recorded as a contribution automatically.
- **Check-in agendas & acknowledgements:** Add prompts and follow-up items to a household check-in, and let members confirm they've read a house guideline. A confirmation is a household record, not a legal agreement.
- **iPadOS native adaptation:** A multi-column tablet layout with sidebar navigation, side-by-side ledger details, keyboard shortcuts, and Stage Manager multitasking support.
- **iPhone Duo dual-screen experience:** Layout and navigation support for foldable displays, including seamless outer-to-inner screen continuity, tabletop mode, and hinge-aware safe areas.
