<Tip>
  We shape Kouchd around the everyday details that make shared living easier to coordinate. Kouchd is currently in **prerelease** (version 0.8) while we prepare for the App Store. We're focused on reliability, accessibility, and stability, and we list new capabilities below without promised dates.
</Tip>

## Available in the current web app

- **Individual guest agreements:** Hosts can customize dates, daily or weekly contribution rates, grace periods, and late-fee rules per guest while keeping house guidelines shared. Guests review and accept the agreement that applies to them.
- **Delayed agreement review window:** Hosts can choose an automatic acceptance window (such as 24 hours) for pending agreements, or turn off automatic acceptance so each guest reviews and confirms terms before joining. Guests can accept immediately at any time.
- **Display names across the household:** Household members see clear member names synced from account profiles across the dashboard, ledger, and chore boards.
- **Shared guidelines & chores:** Household expectations for quiet hours, kitchen cleanup, visitors, and shared spaces, plus a household chore board with due dates and assignments.
- **Transparent contribution records:** Track outside payments, groceries, supplies, and shared housework with notes, confirmation statuses, and bilateral privacy between each guest and host.
- **Host-shared payment options:** Hosts can share direct payment handles or links for PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay. Guests pay outside Kouchd using their preferred method.
- **Email & text reminders:** Reminders reflect due dates, grace periods, and current balances. Email and text reminders are controlled by separate opt-in preferences with standard STOP and HELP controls.
- **Calm, accessible design:** A gentle Liquid Glass interface with fluid transitions, warm surfaces, and high contrast designed to keep household coordination stress-free.
- **Community updates:** An optional stay-tips newsletter for hosts and guests.
- **Protected door and Wi-Fi codes:** Guests see door and Wi-Fi codes only after their agreement is accepted, and until the stay ends. Hosts always see their own codes.
- **Log a purchase from the shopping list:** After checking off a shared item, choose **Log contribution** to start a contribution with the item filled in. You enter the amount yourself, and a host with several guests picks whose Ledger it goes on.

## What Kouchd does not handle

<Warning>
  **In-app payment processing:** Kouchd does not collect, hold, transfer, or process payments. Hosts and guests arrange payments directly using external payment methods they choose.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Stay disputes & conflict resolution:** Kouchd is a shared coordination and record-keeping tool. Kouchd does not mediate, arbitrate, or handle personal disagreements, house rule conflicts, or living situation disputes between hosts and guests. Household members are responsible for communicating with each other and resolving disagreements directly.
</Warning>

## In progress (Pre-1.0 stabilization)

- **Release candidate hardening:** Final pre-lock merges for dependency security updates, package maintenance, and launch candidate checks.
- **Household utility & bill splitting on Plus (in development):** Hosts on Plus can split shared household bills — such as electricity, water, gas, internet, or trash — equally, by percentage, or by fixed amount, with optional proration based on each guest's stay dates. Posting a bill adds a shared record to each included guest's private ledger without moving money directly, and each guest sees only their own share.
- **iPad layout (in development):** A layout made for the larger screen, with sidebar navigation, multi-column screens, side-by-side Ledger details, and keyboard shortcuts. It adjusts as you resize the window in Split View or Stage Manager.
- **Accessibility & keyboard flow:** Refinements to keyboard focus, screen-reader labels, and mobile tap targets across all modals and cards.
- **Shared shopping lists & quick contribution bridge:** A list for each stay with quantities, notes, and shared or personal items, plus a one-tap shortcut to log a purchased item directly into the private ledger.
- **Repeating chores with automatic turn rotation:** Set up daily or weekly chores, with an option to take turns automatically across the household. When someone checks off their turn, the next occurrence is automatically scheduled for the next person in line.
- **Household check-ins:** A host and guest can set a check-in date and topic, add responses, and mark it for follow-up or resolved. Guests see only their own check-ins.
- **Log a purchase from the shopping list on iPhone (in development):** A similar shortcut for iPhone is being built and isn't available yet. At first it will be offered to guests and to hosts with one guest.
- **Plus on iPhone:** In the iPhone development preview, hosts can see their guest spots and move to Plus from inside the app. Guests always join free.
- **iPhone introduction and sign-in polish:** In the iPhone development preview, swipe between the introduction screens, and sign in or create an account on screens that match the rest of Kouchd.

## Being explored (Post-1.0 roadmap)

- **Receipt photos for shared expenses:** Snap a photo of a store receipt when you record a grocery run or household supply contribution, giving everyone a clear receipt to look back on.
- **Split shared household costs:** Divide shared purchases like groceries or cleaning supplies evenly across household members, with everyone's individual contribution record kept private with the host.
- **Year-end ledger and tax exports:** Download a clean summary of your confirmed contributions in CSV or PDF format for taxes or personal household budgeting.
- **Chore turn skips & trades:** Easily pass your turn on a rotating chore if you are traveling or busy, without breaking the house rhythm.
- **Household supplies:** Keep track of what's on hand, such as paper towels or dish soap, and get a nudge to add it to the shopping list when it runs low. Nothing is bought or recorded as a contribution automatically.
- **Check-in agendas & acknowledgements:** Add prompts and follow-up items to a household check-in, and let members confirm they've read a house guideline. A confirmation is a household record, not a legal agreement.
- **Simplified introduction page:** A shorter overview of Kouchd for people who want the essentials at a glance.
- **Native iPhone App Store release:** Expanding the current native SwiftUI development preview to full App Store distribution and push notifications.
- **iPhone Duo dual-screen experience:** Layout and navigation support for foldable displays, including seamless outer-to-inner screen continuity, tabletop mode, and hinge-aware safe areas.
