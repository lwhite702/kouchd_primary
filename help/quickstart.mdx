## Sign in

Open [Kouchd](https://kouchd.com) and sign in or create an account.

## Host a stay

1. Choose **Host a Stay** and enter the planned dates, daily or weekly rate, and household rules.
2. Review the terms, rewards, and any late-fee or discount rules before creating the stay.
3. Add optional PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay details in **Stay**. These are links or contact details you provide; Kouchd does not process payments.
4. Invite each guest with a private link or share the household code. To text an invite, copy the link and paste it into a message on your phone. Kouchd does not send the text or access your contacts.

Each guest has their own agreement and private ledger. New guests start with a copy of the household defaults, which you can customize for that guest.

## Join as a guest

1. Open the link your host shared or choose **Join with Code** and enter the household code.
2. Review the host, stay dates, shared house rules, and your individual contribution terms.
3. Ask your host about anything that is unclear, then accept your agreement to finish joining.

## Follow contributions

Use **Home** to see confirmed payment progress and your current balance. Use **Ledger** to review charges and record contributions. Payment happens outside Kouchd; entries count toward the ledger after confirmation.

Use **Chores** for shared tasks and **Stay** for agreement, household details, and the host’s payment options.
