## 1. Sign In to Kouchd
Visit [kouchd.com](https://kouchd.com) and sign in using your email address or Google account. Authentication is secured by Clerk.

## 2. If You Are a Host
1. Complete the onboarding wizard to define your stay:
   - **Dates**: Start date and target departure date.
   - **Rate & Cadence**: Daily or weekly rate (e.g. $200 / week).
   - **Incentives**: Early payment discount (e.g. 10% off if paid 2 days early) and late fees.
   - **House Rules**: Kitchen guidelines, quiet hours, and visitor policies.
   - **Contribution Rewards**: Enable custom coins (e.g. Kouchd Coins) to reward household help with stay credits.
2. Share your private invitation link or readable household referral code (e.g. `KCH-LAKE-7429`) with your guest.

## 3. If You Are a Guest
1. Click the invitation link sent by your host, or enter the household referral code on your dashboard.
2. Review the proposed rates, schedule, and living expectations.
3. Click **Accept & Co-sign Agreement** to activate the stay.

![Kouchd Household Workspace](/help/images/dashboard-overview.png)

## 4. Stay in Sync
Once active:
- Check the **Home** tab for upcoming dues, check-ins, and announcements.
- Head to **Ledger & Contributions** to log payments, grocery receipts, household supplies, or redeem earned reward coins.
- Check **Chores** to see who is taking out the bins or wiping down the kitchen counters.
