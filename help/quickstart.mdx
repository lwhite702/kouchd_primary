![Kouchd stay overview at a glance](/help/images/dashboard-overview.png)

Open [Kouchd](https://app.kouchd.com) and sign in or create an account.

## Host a stay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the stay">
    Choose **Host a new stay**, then set the name, dates, and contribution schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set house rules">
    Pick the expectations that matter, like quiet hours and visitors, or write your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on rewards and fees">
    Rewards, early-payment discounts, and late fees are all optional. Add how guests can pay you here too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and invite">
    Accept the agreement, then share your join code or a private link. [More on inviting](/help/hosts/inviting-guests).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Join as a guest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invite">
    Tap your host's link, or choose **Join with code** and enter their code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    Make sure the host, dates, and rate match what you discussed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and accept">
    Read the house rules and your own terms, then choose **Accept Terms & Enter Household**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find your way around

- **Home:** What needs attention, announcements, and recent activity.
- **Ledger:** Charges, contributions, and balances.
- **Chores:** Who's doing what, and by when.
- **Stay:** Agreements, house rules, and how to pay your host.
- **Shopping list, House check-ins, and House guide:** In the web sidebar, or under **More** and **Stay** on iPhone.

New here? Choose **Tour** in the app header for a short guided walk-through. You can take it again anytime.

<Note>
  Money moves directly between host and guest, outside Kouchd. The app keeps a shared record of it.
</Note>
