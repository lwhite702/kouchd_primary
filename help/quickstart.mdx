<Tip>
  Welcome to Kouchd! Whether you're hosting a guest or staying with someone, Kouchd keeps expectations, house rules, and outside payment records transparent.
</Tip>

## Access Kouchd

Open the web app at [app.kouchd.com](https://app.kouchd.com) or download the native iOS app for iPhone and iPad. Sign in or create an account using your email or phone number.

## Host a stay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the stay">
    Choose **Host a Stay** and enter your planned stay dates, contribution cadence (daily or weekly), and house guidelines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure house guidelines & terms">
    Review the terms, chore guidelines, household notes, and any late-fee or discount rules before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add payment methods">
    Add your preferred payment details for PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay in **Stay**. These are direct handles or links you share; Kouchd does not hold or process funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite guests">
    Share the private household code or copy a direct invite link to text or email each guest. Each guest receives their own agreement and private ledger.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Join as a guest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite">
    Open the link your host shared or choose **Join with Code** on the homepage and enter the household code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the household guidelines">
    Review your host, stay dates, shared house expectations, and quiet hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept your agreement">
    Review your individual contribution amount and cadence. Once everything is clear, accept your agreement to complete joining.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow contributions

<Info>
  **External payments, shared records:** All money moves directly between host and guest outside Kouchd. The app provides a mutual record where contributions are logged and confirmed.
</Info>

Use **Home** to see confirmed contribution progress and your active balance. Use **Ledger** to review charges and log payments made outside the app. Use **Chores** to coordinate household responsibilities.
