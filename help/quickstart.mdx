<Tip>
  Welcome to Kouchd! Whether you are hosting a housemate or staying with someone, Kouchd keeps expectations, house guidelines, and outside contribution records transparent and calm.
</Tip>

![Kouchd stay overview at a glance](/help/images/dashboard-overview.png)

## Sign in

Open [Kouchd](https://kouchd.com) and sign in or create an account with your email or phone number.

## Host a stay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the stay">
    Choose **Host a Stay** and enter your planned stay dates, contribution cadence (daily or weekly), and rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure house guidelines & notes">
    Set quiet hours, kitchen cleanup expectations, visitor guidelines, and any household notes before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add payment methods">
    Add your direct handles or links for PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay in **Stay**. Kouchd shares these handles with confirmed household members so they can send payments directly outside the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite guests">
    Share your private household code (e.g. `KCH-OAK-4821`) or copy a direct invite link to send to each guest. Each guest receives their own agreement and private ledger.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Join as a guest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invite">
    Open the link your host shared or choose **Join with Code** on the homepage and enter the household code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and join household">
    Review the host name, dates, and rate in the code preview, then choose **Join Household**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review guidelines and accept terms">
    Review full quiet hours, kitchen standards, and your individual contribution rate and cadence. When everything is clear, select **Accept Terms & Enter Household**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow contributions

<Info>
  **Outside payments, shared records:** All money moves directly between host and guest outside Kouchd. The app provides a mutual record where contributions are logged and confirmed.
</Info>

- Use **Home** to see confirmed contribution progress and your active balance.
- Use **Ledger** to review charges and log payments made outside the app.
- Use **Chores** to coordinate household responsibilities.
- Use **Stay** to reference house guidelines and payment handles anytime.

<Note>
  An iPhone app with the same Home, Ledger, Chores, and Stay tabs is in development. It isn't on the App Store yet. Preview how it works in [Using Kouchd on iPhone](/help/ios/using-kouchd-on-ios).
</Note>
