## 1. Sign In
Head over to [kouchd.com](https://kouchd.com) and log in with your email address or Google account.

## 2. If You're Hosting
1. Follow the quick setup wizard to shape your stay:
   - **Dates**: Pick your arrival and target departure dates.
   - **Rates**: Set a friendly daily or weekly amount (like $200/week).
   - **Discounts**: Add an early-bird discount or grace period if you like.
   - **House Guidelines**: Pick standard guidelines for kitchen cleanup, quiet hours, and visitors.
   - **House Rewards**: Turn on custom coins (like *Kouchd Coins*) to reward house help with stay credits.
2. Text your private invite link or 3-part household code (like `KCH-LAKE-7429`) to your guest.

## 3. If You're a Guest
1. Click the invite link your host sent you, or tap **Join with Code** and enter the 3-part code.
2. Review the dates, house rules, and weekly share.
3. Tap **Accept Agreement** to step inside.

![Kouchd Household Workspace](/help/images/dashboard-overview.png)

## 4. Stay Connected
Once your stay is active:
- Check **Home** for upcoming dues, check-ins, and house announcements.
- Head to **Ledger** to log cash payments, grocery receipts, or turn earned reward coins into credits.
- Check **Chores** to see who’s taking out the recycling or wiping down the counters.
