---
title: Email and text reminders
description: Choose how Kouchd can send optional stay and household reminders.
---

Kouchd can send transactional reminders about household activity. Email and SMS are separate choices. Message timing and availability can vary.

## Change your preferences

1. Sign in to Kouchd.
2. Open **Notification settings** from the settings button in the app header.
3. Choose whether to receive email reminders.
4. To receive texts, add a mobile number and check the separate SMS consent box.
5. Save your preferences.

SMS is optional and is off until you choose it. Checking the box means you agree to receive transactional text messages from Wrelik Brands LLC, which operates Kouchd. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. Consent is not a condition of a stay.

## What reminders cover

Depending on your role, stay activity, and selected channels, reminders can include an upcoming check-in, a contribution due date, a chore due date, or a one-time household charge. Kouchd may also send an email invitation when a host chooses email delivery.

Kouchd records contributions and agreements; it does not collect or process the payment itself. A reminder does not mean that money moved or that a payment was received.

## Turn off texts or get help

- Turn off **SMS reminders** in **Notification settings** and save.
- You can also reply **STOP** to a Kouchd text to opt out.
- Reply **HELP** for assistance or email [support@wrelik.com](mailto:support@wrelik.com).
- Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Turning off email does not change your SMS choice, and turning off SMS does not change your email choice.

<Note>
Reminders depend on your saved preferences and notification service availability. Check your Kouchd account if you need the latest stay information.
</Note>
