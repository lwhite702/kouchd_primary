# Make Room for Clarity

**Kouchd** is the friendly household organizer designed to take the awkwardness out of sharing a place. Whether you’re crashing on a friend’s couch for three weeks or staying in a spare room while starting a new gig, Kouchd keeps expectations clear, money conversations easy, and friendships strong.

## The Kouchd Space

![Kouchd Dashboard Overview](/help/images/dashboard-overview.png)

Kouchd gives you a clean, simple dashboard on phone or computer where hosts and guests can check balances, upcoming dates, house notes, and chores at a single glance.

## What We Believe In

- **Clear, simple math**: Every stay charge, early discount, and grocery credit is calculated down to the penny.
- **Both people agree**: A stay only starts when both host and guest take a look and agree to the plan.
- **Credit for everyday help**: Non-cash help—like doing a grocery run, grabbing toilet paper, or deep-cleaning the kitchen—counts just like cash.
- **Honest shared history**: Confirmed payments and agreed guidelines can’t be secretly erased or edited behind someone's back.
