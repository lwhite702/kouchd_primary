# Make Room for Clarity

**Kouchd** is the collaborative household ledger and stay agreement platform designed to eliminate the awkwardness of informal hosting. Whether you are hosting a friend on your couch for three weeks or staying in a spare room while transitioning jobs, Kouchd keeps expectations clear, finances transparent, and friendships intact.

## The Kouchd Workspace

![Kouchd Dashboard Overview](/help/images/dashboard-overview.png)

Kouchd provides a calm, unified desktop and mobile dashboard where hosts and guests can check balances, upcoming milestones, recent household activity, and task status at a single glance.

## Core Principles

- **Transparent math**: Every obligation, early discount, late fee, and partial payment is calculated deterministically down to the exact cent.
- **Mutual consent**: Stays do not become active until both the host and the guest accept the identical, versioned terms.
- **Fair credit for real help**: Non-cash contributions like grocery runs, household supplies, and deep cleaning are tracked and credited alongside cash payments.
- **Immutable audit history**: Confirmed ledger entries and terms cannot be silently modified or erased.
