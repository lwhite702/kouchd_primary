## Keep the record clear

Use **Home** for household updates and confirmed contribution progress. Use **Ledger** for individual charges, contributions, and balances. **Chores** keeps shared tasks visible, and **Stay** contains agreement, household, and host-provided payment details.

Each guest has a separate agreement and private ledger. Kouchd records contributions made outside the app; it does not collect or process payments.
