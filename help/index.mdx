## Keep the record clear

Use **Home** for household updates and confirmed contribution progress. Use **Ledger** for individual charges, contributions, and balances. **Chores** keeps shared tasks visible, and **Stay** contains agreement, household, and host-provided payment details.

<Note>
  **Outside payments & shared records:** Each guest has a separate agreement and private ledger. Kouchd records contributions made outside the app; it does not collect or process payments.
</Note>

<Info>
  **App support vs. stay disagreements:** For technical assistance with your account or the app, email [help@kouchd.com](mailto:help@kouchd.com). Please note that Kouchd is a shared organizer and does not mediate roommate conflicts, personal stay disagreements, or house rule disputes.
</Info>
