  Kouchd is an agreement and shared bookkeeping tool operated by Wrelik Brands LLC. It is not a landlord, tenant agency, real estate broker, or payment processor.
</Tip>

## Platform role and core boundaries

Kouchd helps hosts and temporary houseguests establish mutual expectations, house rules, and transparent contribution ledgers:

- **Not a landlord or leasing agency:** Kouchd does not own, lease, manage, inspect, or broker real estate. It does not replace local residential tenancy laws, building lease rules, or legal counsel.
- **Informational ledger only:** Kouchd does not collect, hold, escrow, custody, transfer, or process user payments. Any financial settlement is conducted externally and directly between stay members.
- **Mutual agreement required:** Stay terms, rate changes, chore assignments, and ledger contribution entries require mutual review and confirmation to maintain transparent household records.

## Individual stay agreements

Each guest has their own agreement and private ledger. A host can customize a guest’s dates, rate, cadence, discounts, grace period, and late-fee terms without changing another guest’s agreement. House rules remain shared by default.

Each guest accepts their own agreement. If the host revises that guest’s terms, the guest reviews and accepts the revision before it takes effect for them.

## Customer support and dispute boundaries

Kouchd provides technical customer support at **help@kouchd.com** for app usability, login issues, bug reports, and account preferences:

- **App support only:** Contact `help@kouchd.com` for technical questions about using the app, reporting software defects, or managing account data.
- **No mediation or conflict resolution:** Kouchd is strictly a shared coordination tool. Kouchd does not mediate, arbitrate, investigate, or resolve interpersonal disputes, house rule violations, property maintenance issues, or personal disagreements between hosts and guests.
- **No financial enforcement:** Kouchd does not process, hold, or collect money, and does not enforce external payment agreements or issue refunds.
- **Direct resolution:** All interpersonal disagreements and living arrangement questions must be resolved directly between household members.

## SMS terms of service

When you provide a mobile phone number and opt into SMS alerts:

- **Program description:** Transactional text alerts regarding stay agreement updates, mutual sign-offs, chore deadline reminders, check-in logistics, and household ledger updates.
- **Rates and charges:** Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for standard text messaging rates.
- **Frequency:** Message frequency varies based on household stay activity.
- **Opt-out (STOP):** Text **STOP**, **UNSUBSCRIBE**, or **QUIT** to any message from Kouchd to cancel.
- **Support (HELP):** Text **HELP** to any message, or email support@kouchd.com or support@wrelik.com.
- **Privacy protection:** We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
