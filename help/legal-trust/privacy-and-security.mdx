---
title: Privacy, data, and security
description: Who can see household information, agreements, and financial records in Kouchd.
---

## Sign-in and account access

Kouchd uses its configured sign-in provider to authenticate accounts. Keep your account credentials private and use the account recovery options provided by that service.

## Household visibility

Household members can see shared stay information, house rules, chores, and announcements. A guest’s agreement and financial ledger are visible to that guest and the host. Other guests do not see that guest’s private financial details.

Private invite links may reveal the host’s name in link previews so recipients can recognize the invitation. Do not post private invite links publicly. Invite links expire.

## Payment details and records

A host may share PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay contact details with household members. Those details direct the guest to an outside payment service or contact method. Kouchd does not access bank accounts or collect, hold, transfer, or process funds. A contribution entry records what household members report and confirm; it does not independently verify an external transaction.

## Activity history

Kouchd records household contributions, confirmations, and other supported activity for the shared record. Use the in-app agreement and privacy controls to understand who can see each item.
