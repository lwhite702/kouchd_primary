## Data Privacy & Encryption

1. **Authentication & Identity**:
   - Authentication is powered by Clerk with enterprise-grade encryption and security practices.
   - We do not store passwords in plain text or handle raw credentials.
2. **Convex Realtime Database**:
   - Stays and household ledgers are completely partitioned. Only verified members of a household stay can view or query records associated with that stay.
3. **No Direct Fund Custody**:
   - Kouchd does not hold or escrow user funds. Users transfer funds externally (via bank transfer, cash, or payment apps) and log the transaction details on Kouchd for shared bookkeeping.
4. **Audit Trails**:
   - Every financial action (creation, confirmation, dispute, waiver, or void) creates an immutable entry in the stay activity timeline with a timestamp and the actor's user ID.
