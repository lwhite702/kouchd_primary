<Tip>
  Privacy by design: Each guest's financial terms, payment records, and ledger remain strictly between that guest and the stay host.
</Tip>

## Sign-in and account access

Kouchd uses a sign-in provider (Clerk) to authenticate accounts. Keep your account credentials private and use the account recovery options provided by that service.

## Household visibility

Household members can see shared stay information, house rules, shared chores, shared shopping items, the house guide (including Wi-Fi and door codes), and announcements. Personal shopping items are visible only to the person who added them, and check-ins only to that guest and the host. A guest’s agreement and financial ledger are visible to that guest and the host. Other guests do not see that guest’s private financial details.

Private invite links use hashed tokens and may reveal the host’s name in link previews so recipients can recognize the invitation. Do not post private invite links publicly. Invite links expire automatically.

## Payment details and records

<Info>
  **Zero bank access:** Kouchd does not connect to bank accounts or process financial transactions. Contribution entries are mutual records agreed upon and confirmed by stay participants.
</Info>

A host may share Venmo, Cash App, Chime, Zelle, PayPal, or Apple Pay contact details with household members. Those details direct the guest to an outside payment service or contact method. Kouchd does not access bank accounts or collect, hold, transfer, or process funds. A contribution entry records what household members report and confirm; it does not independently verify an external transaction.

## SMS communications and privacy commitment

Wrelik Brands LLC maintains a strict privacy policy for SMS notifications:

- **Zero third-party marketing sharing:** We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
- **Transactional notifications only:** Text messages are used solely to deliver requested operational notices, such as chore reminders, stay agreement updates, and check-in logistics.
- **Opt-out and support:** Reply **STOP** to cancel text alerts at any time, or **HELP** for customer support assistance. Message and data rates may apply.
