## Keeping Your Info Safe

Here’s how we look after your privacy and keep your details protected—in plain, simple terms:

1. **Safe & Private Sign-In**:
   - We use Clerk to handle secure sign-ins.
   - We never see, store, or hold your passwords in the clear. Your login stays between you and your authentication provider.

2. **Your Household Stays Between You**:
   - Your stay details and money tabs are completely private.
   - Only the people in your stay can see your shared info. Nobody outside your household has access.
   - If a host has multiple guests, each guest's financial tab is strictly between that guest and the host.

3. **We Never Hold Your Money**:
   - Kouchd doesn’t hold onto your cash or touch your bank accounts.
   - You and your host settle up directly however you prefer—like Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or plain cash.
   - Kouchd is just your friendly shared notebook so you both agree on what’s paid, what’s credited, and what’s due.

4. **Clear History, Zero Surprises**:
   - Whenever someone logs a payment, confirms groceries, or adjusts a fee, Kouchd notes who did it and when.
   - Past entries can’t be secretly erased or edited behind your back, so everyone always sees the same honest story.
