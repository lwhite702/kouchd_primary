## What a Stay Agreement Is All About

Kouchd is simply a shared space to make extended stays, couch surfing, and living together easier and drama-free.

### Good to Know
- **We’re a friendly organizer, not landlords or lawyers**: Kouchd isn’t a real estate company, landlord agency, or lease broker. We’re here to help friends and housemates communicate clearly.
- **A shared record you both trust**: When you both accept a stay, it’s simply a digital handshake saying: "Yes, we’re on the same page about dates, chores, and costs."
- **Checking local rules**: If you’re staying somewhere for a long time, it’s always a good idea to check your local city rules on house guests and shared living.

### Changing Things Down the Road
- Plans change—we get it! If your host wants to update dates, house rules, or rates, they’ll propose an updated version.
- Nothing updates automatically behind your back. Both the host and the guest review and say yes to the new version before it kicks in.
