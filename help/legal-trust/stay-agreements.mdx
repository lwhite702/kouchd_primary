---
title: Stay agreements and house rules
description: How individual guest terms and shared household rules work in Kouchd.
---

Kouchd helps hosts and guests keep an agreed record of stay dates, contribution terms, and house rules. It is a shared organizer, not a payment processor or legal service.

## Individual agreements

Each guest has their own agreement and private ledger. New guests receive a copy of the household defaults. A host can customize a guest’s dates, rate, cadence, discounts, grace period, and late-fee terms without changing another guest’s agreement. House rules remain shared by default.

Each guest accepts their own agreement. If the host revises that guest’s terms, the guest reviews and accepts the revision before it takes effect for them.

## Payment records

Kouchd displays payment details supplied by the host and records contributions that household members report and confirm. Payments take place through external services or other arrangements between the host and guest. Kouchd does not collect, hold, transfer, or process funds, and ledger records do not independently verify an external transaction.

## Talk through changes

Use the agreement and household notes to make expectations clear. Discuss proposed changes directly with the affected guest before updating terms. Check local requirements that may apply to your living arrangement.
