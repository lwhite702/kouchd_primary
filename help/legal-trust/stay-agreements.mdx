## Agreement Nature

Kouchd provides an agreement and record-keeping tool for informal cohabitation, extended visits, and mutual household support.

### Important Distinctions
- **Not a Lease or Brokerage**: Kouchd is not a real estate broker, leasing agent, landlord-tenant arbitration board, or escrow holder.
- **Mutual Record**: The terms accepted on Kouchd serve as documentary evidence of mutual intent and shared understanding between host and guest.
- **Local Tenancy Laws**: Users should consult local municipal laws regarding extended guest stays, subletting, and residential guest occupancy limits.

### Versioning & Immutability
- All stay terms are versioned (`v1`, `v2`, etc.).
- A host cannot modify rates, due dates, or rules without creating a new terms version.
- Any new version requires explicit co-signing by both the host and the guest before taking effect.
