---
title: Using reminders
description: Choose reminder channels and understand what Kouchd reminders mean.
---

Open the settings icon in your Kouchd app header to find **Reminder settings**. Email and SMS preferences are separate. SMS is optional and requires a mobile number and your consent; message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. Reply **STOP** to opt out or **HELP** for assistance.

Payment reminders may reflect an upcoming due date, the due date itself, an accepted grace period, a late-fee term, and the current ledger. Their timing depends on your stay, the accepted agreement, your time zone, saved preferences, and notification service availability. A reminder is not proof that a payment was processed or received.

See [Email and text reminders](/help/notifications/notification-preferences) for detailed preference and opt-out steps.
