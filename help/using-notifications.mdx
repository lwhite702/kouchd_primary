---
title: Reminders and notification settings
description: Choose how Kouchd sends household reminders to you.
---

Open **Settings** in your household workspace to review your email and SMS reminder preferences. SMS is optional and requires a phone number and your consent. You can turn notification channels off in settings.

Reminders are based on the agreement and ledger information available in Kouchd. Check your ledger for the current charge, confirmed contributions, and balance. Kouchd does not process or verify outside payments.
