---
title: "Inviting guests"
description: "Share a join code or a private link, and see what guests see before they join."
---

You can invite people in two ways. Both start in **Stay**, under **Invite members**.

## Join code

Every stay has a code that looks like `KCH-7QH2MP`. Guests choose **Join with code**, enter it, and see a preview of your stay before they join.

Need to stop an old code from working? Choose **New code**. The old one stops working, so share the new one with anyone still joining.

## Private invite link

Copy a link and text it, or enter a guest's email and Kouchd sends it for you.

- Links made in the web app expire after 7 days.
- Each link works once.
- A link can show your name in a message preview, so people know who it's from. Don't post it publicly.

## What happens next

1. The guest signs in or creates an account.
2. They join, and their agreement starts as a copy of your stay's terms.
3. They review the house rules and terms, then accept. See [automatic acceptance](/help/hosts/getting-started#automatic-acceptance) if you want a time limit.

If your plan has no open guest spot, the guest is told the stay is full. See [Plans and limits](/help/hosts/plans-and-limits).
