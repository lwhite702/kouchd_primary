Your stay gives everyone one calm place for expectations, agreements, chores, and contribution records. Each guest gets their own agreement and private Ledger.

## Create your stay

Choose **Host a new stay** and work through five short steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stay schedule">
    Name the stay and set its start date, planned end, contribution schedule and rate, first check-in date, and time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Living expectations">
    Choose house rules or add your own. Everyone in the stay shares these.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contribution rewards (optional)">
    Let guests earn coins for approved help. See [Kouchd Coins](/help/guests/kouchd-coins).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Financial rules (optional)">
    Offer an early-payment discount or set a late fee and grace period. Both are off by default. Add how guests can pay you, too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final agreement">
    Review everything and accept it. You can also opt in to text alerts here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Host setup - house guidelines](/help/images/host-wizard-rules.png)

<Info>
  Kouchd shows guests your payment details, but it doesn't handle money. Guests pay you directly.
</Info>

## Customize a guest's agreement

Each guest starts with a copy of your stay's terms. To change one, open **Stay**, find the guest under **Guest terms**, and edit their dates, rate, schedule, discount, or late fee.

House rules stay shared. Money terms stay between you and that guest. If you change a guest's terms, only that guest reviews the update.

## Automatic acceptance

Under **Automatic acceptance** in **Stay**, you can choose what happens when a guest doesn't respond:

- **On:** Pending agreements are accepted after a wait you set, from 1 to 720 hours. New stays start at 24 hours.
- **Off:** Each guest has to accept before the agreement takes effect.

Guests can accept right away either way.
