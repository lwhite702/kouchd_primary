<Tip>
  Hosts can manage multiple guests in a stay. Each guest has a private ledger and an agreement that can have its own dates, rate, cadence, and financial terms.
</Tip>

Kouchd gives your household one place for expectations, agreements, chores, and contribution records.

## Set up the household

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose stay cadence & rates">
    Choose **Host a Stay**, give the stay a recognizable name, and enter the dates, daily or weekly cadence, and rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add house guidelines & notes">
    Add shared house rules, quiet hours, household notes, and optional rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define financial rules">
    Set any discount, grace-period, or late-fee rules and review the terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add external payment options">
    Add payment details for any combination of PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, and Apple Pay. Enter the username, contact, or HTTPS link guests should use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the stay and invite guests">
    Finalize the stay setup, then share the invite code or private links with your guests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Outside payments:** Payment details are shown only to household members. Kouchd does not handle or process payments directly.
</Info>

## Host Overview & Home Base

Once your stay is set up, the **Host Overview** gives you a single screen to monitor household health across web (`app.kouchd.com`) and iOS:
- **Home Base Hero**: Shows your active household status, dates, and quick actions.
- **Three-Metric Summary Row**: Track **Active Guests**, **Confirmed Contributions**, and pending **Household Chores** at a glance.
- **Needs Attention**: Highlights unaccepted agreements, overdue items, or actions needing host review.
- **Recent Activity**: Chronological timeline of logged contributions, completed chores, and agreement status updates.

## Customize each guest’s agreement

A new guest starts with a copy of the household’s current terms. Open **Stay** and choose **Edit agreement** beside that guest to set their dates, rate, cadence, and individual financial rules. House rules remain shared by default.

Guests see their own agreement and ledger. Each guest accepts their own current terms. If you revise one guest’s agreement, only that guest needs to accept the revision.

## Invite guests

You can share the household code or create a private invite link. To invite by text, copy the link and paste it into a message from your own phone. Kouchd does not send SMS or read your contacts. Invite links expire; check the date shown when you create one before sharing it.

For email invitations, enter the guest’s email in the invite form. Kouchd sends the invitation email with the host name and join link.
