Hosting a friend, acquaintance, or family member should feel comfortable, not fraught with unspoken tension. Kouchd provides the structure so you never have to send an awkward text asking for rent or reminding someone to buy paper towels.

## Setting Up Your Stay

1. **Navigate to the Host Onboarding Wizard**:
   When you first sign in with no active stay, tap **Create a Household Stay**.
2. **Select Stay Dates**:
   Choose the initial arrival date and planned end date. If the end date is flexible, select an initial target check-in review date.
3. **Set Financial Expectations**:
   - Set a daily or weekly contribution amount.
   - Choose whether you want to offer an **Early Contribution Discount** (e.g. 10% discount if paid 2 days prior to the due date).
   - Set an optional **Late Fee** with grace days.
4. **Define House Rules & Boundaries**:
   Select from standard household guidelines (e.g., quiet hours after 10 PM, clean dishes immediately, guest visitor policy) or write your own custom rules.

![Host Onboarding Wizard - House Guidelines](/help/images/host-wizard-rules.png)

5. **Configure Contribution Rewards (Optional)**:
   Choose whether to enable household coins (e.g. Kouchd Coins), select starter tasks, and establish a conversion rate for stay credits.

## Inviting Your Guest

Once you complete the setup, Kouchd generates two seamless ways to bring your guest into the household:
- **Household Referral Code**: A human-readable code (e.g. `KCH-LAKE-7429`) your guest can enter directly from their dashboard.
- **Direct Invite Link**: A secure, single-use URL you can text or message directly.

Until your guest reviews and accepts the terms via the guided onboarding flow, the stay remains in `Pending Acceptance` mode.
