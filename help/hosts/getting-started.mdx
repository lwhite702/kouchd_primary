Opening your home to a friend, family member, or coworker should feel good—not awkward. Kouchd keeps everything organized so you never have to send uncomfortable texts asking for money or reminding someone to buy paper towels.

## Setting Up Your Stay

1. **Tap Host a Stay**:
   When you first log in, tap **Host a Stay** to start the setup.
2. **Pick Your Dates**:
   Choose when your guest is arriving and about when they're heading out. If plans are loose, pick an initial check-in date to touch base.
3. **Set Up the Money Details**:
   - Pick a daily or weekly contribution amount.
   - Choose whether you want to offer an **Early Discount** (like 10% off if paid 2 days early).
   - Add an optional small **Late Fee** with a couple of grace days.
4. **Share House Rules & Comforts**:
   Pick standard guidelines (like quiet hours after 10 PM, washing dishes promptly, or a heads-up before friends visit) or add your own custom house notes.

![Host Setup - House Guidelines](/help/images/host-wizard-rules.png)

5. **Set Up House Rewards (Optional)**:
   Want to reward your guest for cooking dinners or vacuuming? Turn on household coins (like *Kouchd Coins*), pick a few starter chores, and set what they're worth toward the stay.

## Inviting Your Guest

Once you finish setup, Kouchd gives you two super easy ways to connect:
- **Household Code**: A simple 3-part code (like `KCH-LAKE-7429`) your guest can type right into Kouchd.
- **Direct Link**: A private link you can text or email right to them.

Until your guest opens the link and agrees to the house plan, the stay is marked `Pending Acceptance`.
