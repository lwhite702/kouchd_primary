---
title: Getting started as a host
description: Set up a stay, create clear guest agreements, and invite people to join.
---

Kouchd gives your household one place for expectations, agreements, chores, and contribution records. You can have multiple guests in a stay. Each guest has a private ledger and an agreement that can have its own dates, rate, cadence, and financial rules.

## Set up the household

1. Choose **Host a Stay** and enter the dates, daily or weekly cadence, and rate.
2. Add shared house rules, notes, and optional rewards.
3. Set any discount, grace-period, or late-fee rules and review the terms.
4. Add payment details for any combination of PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, and Apple Pay. Enter the username, contact, or HTTPS link guests should use. These details are shown only to household members. Kouchd does not handle or process payments.
5. Create the stay, then invite guests.

![Host setup - house guidelines](/help/images/host-wizard-rules.png)

## Customize each guest’s agreement

A new guest starts with a copy of the household’s current terms. Open **Stay** and choose **Edit agreement** beside that guest to set their dates, rate, cadence, and individual financial rules. House rules remain shared by default.

Guests see their own agreement and ledger. Each guest accepts their own current terms. If you revise one guest’s agreement, only that guest needs to accept the revision.

## Invite guests

You can share the household code or create a private invite link. To invite by text, copy the link and paste it into a message from your own phone. Kouchd does not send SMS or read your contacts. Invite links expire; check the date shown when you create one before sharing it.

For email invitations, enter the guest’s email in the invite form. Kouchd sends the invitation email with the host name and join link.
