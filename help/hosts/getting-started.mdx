<Tip>
  Hosts can manage multiple guests in a single stay. Each guest receives their own private ledger and an agreement tailored to their specific stay dates, contribution rates, and cadence.
</Tip>

Kouchd provides your household with a single, calm space for stay expectations, agreements, chores, and contribution bookkeeping.

## Set up your household

When setting up your stay, you will walk through the host onboarding wizard to establish household defaults:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your stay & set dates">
    Choose **Host a Stay**, give your household a recognizable title (such as "Maple Cottage Stay"), and set the overall stay window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Establish house guidelines">
    Select shared expectations for quiet hours, kitchen cleanup, visitor notices, and smoke-free spaces to ensure mutual comfort.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure contribution cadence & rates">
    Set default daily or weekly contribution rates, choose payment days, and define any grace-period or late-fee rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add external payment handles">
    Enter your preferred direct payment handles or links for PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay in **Stay**. Guests use these handles to pay you directly outside Kouchd.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share invite codes & links">
    Generate your private household code (e.g., `KCH-OAK-4821`) or direct invitation links to text or email your guests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Outside payments:** Payment handles are visible only to confirmed household members. Kouchd does not process, handle, or escrow money directly.
</Info>

## Customize each guest’s agreement

Every new guest begins with a copy of your household's default terms. To tailor an individual agreement:

1. Open **Stay** from your navigation.
2. Select **Edit agreement** beside the guest's profile.
3. Adjust their specific stay dates, daily or weekly contribution rate, cadence, and late-fee grace periods.
4. Save your revisions.

House rules remain shared across the home, while financial terms are kept strictly private between you and that guest. If you revise a guest’s terms, only that specific guest reviews and accepts the updated agreement.

## Invite guests to join

You have two ways to invite guests to your household:

- **Private invite links**: Copy a direct join link and paste it into a text message or email from your phone or computer. The link includes cryptographic verification and displays your host name so guests know it is authentic.
- **Household referral code**: Share your stay’s short code (e.g. `KCH-OAK-4821`). Guests can simply enter this code on the Kouchd website to view your stay preview and accept terms.
