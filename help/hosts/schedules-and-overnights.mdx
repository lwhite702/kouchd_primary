<Note>
  These two tools are being rolled out and may not appear in your stay yet. If you don't see them, your stay uses the standard schedule from its agreement.
</Note>

## Schedule change requests

A guest can ask to move between daily and weekly, and choose a weekday for weekly payments.

1. The guest sends a request from **Payment timing**.
2. You review it and approve or reject it.
3. If you approve, the agreement's schedule changes. The daily or weekly rate is adjusted to keep about the same weekly cost.

Once a stay has ended, schedule changes aren't available.

## Occupied nights

Some stays aren't continuous. If an agreement bills by occupied nights, only the overnights you record are charged.

- **Record a night:** Under **Recorded occupancy**, choose the date and save it. It's charged at the agreed rate.
- **Fix a mistake:** You can correct a recorded overnight, which removes its charge.
- **Guests** can see the nights recorded for them.

Talk with your guest about using this before you set it, so you both know how charges will work.
