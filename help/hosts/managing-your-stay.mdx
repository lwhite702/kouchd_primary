title: "Managing your stay"
description: "Rename a stay, choose guest names, share payment options, run more than one stay, and end a stay."
---

## Stay details

Open **Stay** to review dates, terms, and each guest's agreement. From there you can:

- **Rename the stay** so everyone recognizes it.
- **Set a guest's preferred name.** Everyone in the stay sees this name for that guest.
- **Edit the [house guide](/help/household/house-guide)** with Wi-Fi, door, parking, and trash details.

## Share how guests can pay you

Add a username, contact detail, or link for Venmo, Cash App, Chime, Zelle, PayPal, or Apple Pay, or note that you take cash. Guests see these in the app and pay you directly.

<Warning>
  Kouchd only displays these details. It doesn't collect or move money.
</Warning>

## Run more than one stay

Choose **All stays** to switch stays or start a new one. **Active stays** lists what's current, and **Past stays** keeps what's ended. The number of active stays depends on your [plan](/help/hosts/plans-and-limits).

## End a stay

Choose **End this stay** at the bottom of **Stay** and confirm.

- Nobody can add charges, contributions, chores, or check-ins afterward.
- Agreements and history are kept under **Past stays** for everyone to read.
- The stay stops counting against your plan's limit.

<Warning>
  Be sure before you end a stay. New activity stops for the whole household.
</Warning>
