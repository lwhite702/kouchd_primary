## Household defaults and guest terms

The host sets the initial household terms. Each new guest receives a copy of those defaults, and the host can then customize that guest’s dates, daily or weekly rate, cadence, discounts, grace period, and late-fee rules. Shared house rules stay shared by default.

Every guest reviews and accepts their own agreement. A revision to one guest’s financial terms does not change another guest’s agreement or acceptance.

## Review payment progress

The Home and Ledger screens show confirmed contribution progress against charges, the remaining balance, and due dates. Partial contributions count toward the ledger when confirmed. Kouchd applies confirmed credits to the oldest outstanding charge first.

Late fees are the amounts stated in the guest’s accepted terms. Explain the trigger and grace period in the agreement before the stay begins. Hosts can review a fee in the ledger and waive it when appropriate.

## Payments happen outside Kouchd

Hosts can list PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, or Apple Pay details for household members. Guests use those details to pay the host outside Kouchd, then record the completed contribution in the ledger. Kouchd does not collect, hold, transfer, or process money and does not confirm a payment automatically.

## Rewards

Hosts can enable household rewards, name the points, set their credit value, and add a limit. Once approved and redeemed, credits are recorded against the guest’s ledger according to the accepted terms.
