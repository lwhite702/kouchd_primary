This page covers what guests contribute to you. For what Kouchd itself costs, see [Plans and limits](/help/hosts/plans-and-limits).

## What you can set for each guest

- **Stay dates:** When this guest's stay starts and ends.
- **Rate and schedule:** A daily or weekly amount. Weekly agreements have a payment day.
- **Early-payment discount (optional):** A percentage off when someone pays a set number of days ahead.
- **Late fee and grace period (optional):** A fee that applies only after the grace period ends.
- **Billing basis:** Most stays bill continuously. Some can bill by [occupied nights](/help/hosts/schedules-and-overnights).

When a guest first accepts, they can pick daily or weekly. The rate adjusts to keep about the same weekly total.

## Reading progress

**Home** and **Ledger** show total charges, confirmed credit, and what's left.

- **Oldest first:** A confirmed contribution pays down the oldest share first.
- **Partial contributions** count once they're confirmed.
- **Pending entries** don't count until the other person confirms them.

## Late fees

Late fees follow the terms the guest accepted, and don't start until any grace period has passed. You can choose **Waive fee** on any charge, for example if you talked about a delay ahead of time.

## Payments happen outside Kouchd

<Warning>
  Kouchd doesn't collect, hold, or transfer money, and it never connects to a bank account.
</Warning>

Guests pay you using the [payment options you share](/help/hosts/managing-your-stay#share-how-guests-can-pay-you), then record it in Kouchd. The record isn't proof that the money arrived.

## Rewards

If you turn on rewards, guests can earn coins for approved help and redeem them as credit toward their balance. See [Kouchd Coins](/help/guests/kouchd-coins).
