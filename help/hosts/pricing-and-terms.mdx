## Financial Mechanics

Kouchd calculates all ledger items deterministically down to the exact cent using integer arithmetic.

### Cadence
- **Weekly**: Obligations are generated for 7-day intervals starting from your stay's start date.
- **Daily**: Obligations are generated per 24-hour cycle.

### Early Discounts
To encourage timely contributions, you can configure an early discount:
- **Example**: A weekly rate of $200.00 with a 10% discount if paid 2 days early.
- If the obligation is due on September 8th, paying on or before September 6th reduces the effective charge to $180.00.
- The discount is evaluated once per obligation based on the date the full balance or payment is initiated.

### Late Fees & Grace Periods
- **Grace Period**: Number of days after the due date before late fees apply (e.g. 2 grace days).
- **Late Fee**: A flat dollar amount (e.g. $25.00) added to overdue obligations.
- **Host Waiver**: Hosts always retain the ability to waive a late fee with a single tap if circumstances warrant flexibility.

### Contribution Rewards Credits
Hosts can optionally incentivize household contributions by enabling **Contribution Rewards**:
- Set a custom coin name (e.g. *Kouchd Coins* or *Roomie Points*).
- Define the cash conversion rate (e.g. 100 coins = $1.00 credit).
- Set an optional maximum credit limit per period (e.g. $50.00/month maximum).
- When a guest redeems approved points, Kouchd issues an audited credit that reduces the guest's oldest outstanding obligation in the ledger via FIFO.
