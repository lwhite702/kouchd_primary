## How the Money Tab Works

Kouchd keeps the math simple, fair, and clear down to the exact penny.

### Timing
- **Weekly**: Stays are split into simple 7-day intervals starting from move-in day.
- **Daily**: Or you can set a simple per-day rate.

### Early Discounts
Want to encourage your guest to chip in ahead of time? You can set up an optional early discount:
- **Example**: A weekly share of $200.00 with 10% off if paid 2 days early.
- If the week's share is due on Friday, sending it by Wednesday drops it to $180.00.

### Late Fees & Grace Days
- **Grace Period**: Give your guest a couple of days leeway before a late reminder kicks in (e.g., 2 grace days).
- **Late Fee**: A small flat amount (like $20) added if a payment is overdue.
- **Host Flexibility**: Hosts can waive or remove a late fee anytime with one tap if something came up or you worked it out together.

### Earning Credits with Household Help
Hosts can also reward guests for pitching in around the house with **Contribution Rewards**:
- Pick a fun name for your points (like *Kouchd Coins* or *House Points*).
- Choose what points are worth (for instance, 100 points = $1.00 credit).
- Set an optional monthly limit on how much can be earned.
- When points are redeemed, Kouchd automatically applies the credit toward the oldest unpaid share on the tab.
