Plans apply to hosts. Guests are always free and never see plan names or prices.

| | Free | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | $0 | $6.99 a month or $69.99 a year on the website |
| **Active stays** | 1 | Up to 3 |
| **Guests** | 1 across your stays | Up to 5 across your stays |

## How spots are counted

- **Stays:** Active and pending stays count. Ended stays don't.
- **Guests:** Limits cover all your stays together, not each stay. A guest whose agreement is still waiting for review holds a spot.

## When you reach a limit

If you've used every stay, Kouchd explains why and, on Free, offers Plus. To start another stay without upgrading, [end a current one](/help/hosts/managing-your-stay#end-a-stay).

If a guest tries to join and there's no spot, they see that the stay is full and to ask you to make room. On iPhone, you'll see who couldn't join on your subscription screen, and you can dismiss them there.

## Start or manage Plus

- **On the web:** Choose **Host plan** in the app header.
- **On iPhone:** Open **Settings**, then your subscription screen.

A free trial may be offered. Each host account gets one trial across web and iPhone, and the button shows the trial's length before you commit. Kouchd can email you 2 days before a trial ends, and you can turn that reminder off.

Manage or cancel Plus where you bought it. If you started on the website, the iPhone app sends you back there.

<Note>
  Plus pays for your Kouchd plan. It has nothing to do with the contributions you and your guests record, which always happen outside Kouchd.
</Note>
