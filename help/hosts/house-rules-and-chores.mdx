## House rules

You choose house rules when you create a stay, and can add your own. Common ones cover quiet hours, kitchen cleanup, visitors, smoking, and shared supplies.

Everyone in the stay sees the same rules, listed in **Stay**. Changing them creates a new agreement version for guests to review.

## The chore board

**Chores** is your shared to-do list. It shows what's open, who it's for, and when it's due.

![Shared chore board](/help/images/chore-board.png)

## Add a chore

Anyone in the stay can add one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add chore">
    Open **Chores** and choose **Add chore**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and assign it">
    Enter a title and optional details, then choose **Everyone** or a specific person under **Assigned to**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date">
    Pick the date it's due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether it repeats">
    Under **Repeat**, pick **Does not repeat**, **Daily**, or **Weekly**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Check a chore off when it's done, or reopen it if it wasn't.

## Repeating chores

When you finish a repeating chore, the next one is scheduled automatically. If one passes its due date without being done, Kouchd schedules the next one too, so the routine keeps going.

Assigned people may get a [reminder](/help/notifications/notification-preferences) on the due date.

<Note>
  Repeating chores stay with the person you assign. Taking turns automatically isn't available yet, and is on the [roadmap](/help/product/roadmap).
</Note>

<Accordion title="On iPhone" icon="smartphone">
  Tap **Chores**, then **Add chore**. Tap the circle next to a chore to mark it done. See [Chores and stay details on iPhone](/help/ios/chores-and-stay).
</Accordion>
