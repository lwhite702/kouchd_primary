## Living together comfortably

Living together smoothly just takes a little mutual consideration for shared spaces. In your stay setup, you can set guidelines for:

- **Quiet hours**: When it is time to keep things low-key and quiet down music or calls.
- **Kitchen cleanliness**: Keeping dishes out of the sink, cleaning cookware promptly, and wiping down counters.
- **Guests and visitors**: Whether having friends or partners over needs a quick heads-up.
- **Smoking and vaping**: Designated outdoor spots or smoke-free house guidelines.
- **Shared supplies**: How household staples like paper goods, dish soap, and spices are shared or restocked.

## Sharing the chores

The **Chores** tab is your shared to-do board where you and your guest coordinate house tasks:

- **Who is handling it**: Assign tasks to **Host**, **Guest**, or **Everyone**.
- **Due dates**: Clear countdowns like "Due tomorrow" or "Due in 2 days" keep timelines friendly and visible.
- **Done and checked**: Check off a task anytime with a satisfying tap so everyone knows it has been handled.

<Check>
  **Done together:** When a chore is completed, checking it off updates the shared board in real time for everyone in the household.
</Check>

## Add a new chore

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chores">
    Navigate to **Chores** in your navigation bar and choose **+ Add chore**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the task">
    Enter a brief title (such as "Take bins to the curb" or "Wipe down stove counters") and an optional note with helpful details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign responsibility and due date">
    Select who will handle the task (**Host**, **Guest**, or **Everyone**) and pick a target date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Select **Create chore**. The task appears immediately on the household board.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="On iPhone" icon="smartphone">
  1. Tap **Chores**, then **Add a chore** (or **+**).
  2. Enter the chore and any details, choose who it's for (**Shared**, **Host**, or **Guest**), and pick a due date.
  3. Tap **Add**.

  Tap the circle next to a chore to mark it done.

  See [Chores and stay details on iPhone](/help/ios/chores-and-stay). The iPhone app is in development and not yet on the App Store.
</Accordion>
