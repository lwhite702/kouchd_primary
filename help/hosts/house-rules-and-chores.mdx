## Living Together Comfortably

Living together smoothly just takes a little mutual consideration for shared spaces. In your stay setup, you can set guidelines for:
- **Quiet Hours**: When it’s time to keep things low-key and quiet down music or calls.
- **Kitchen Cleanliness**: Keeping dishes out of the sink and wiping counters.
- **Guests & Visitors**: Whether having friends or partners over needs a quick heads-up.
- **Smoking & Vaping**: Outdoor spots or smoke-free house guidelines.

## Sharing the Chores

The **Chores** tab is your shared to-do board where you and your guest can keep track of house tasks:
- **Who's doing it**: Assigned to `Host`, `Guest`, specific members, or `Everyone`.
- **Due Dates**: Simple countdowns like "Due today", "Due tomorrow", or specific calendar dates.
- **Done & Checked**: Check off a task anytime with a satisfying tap so everyone knows it’s handled.
- **Activity & Accountability**: Completed chores are logged in the household activity feed.

<Check>
  **Done together:** When a chore is completed, checking it off updates the shared board in real time for everyone in the household across web and iOS.
</Check>
