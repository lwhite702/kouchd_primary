The Kouchd iPhone app keeps the same shared stay you see on the web: the same agreement, the same Ledger, and the same chores. Anything you record on one shows up on the other.

<Note>
  The iPhone app is still in development and is not yet available on the App Store. We'll share it on the [roadmap](/help/product/roadmap) and [changelog](/help/product/changelog) once it's ready to download. Until then, everything below is also available at [kouchd.com](https://kouchd.com).
</Note>

## Signing in

Open the app and sign in with the same account you use on the web. You can continue with your email address or with Google. Your stays, agreements, and contribution records load automatically.

If you're new, the app shows a short introduction first. At the end, choose **Create a stay** if you're hosting, or **Join a stay** if someone invited you.

## Getting around

The app has four tabs along the bottom of the screen.

<img src="/help/images/ios-home-overview.png" alt="Kouchd iPhone Home tab for a guest, showing the next contribution, an assigned chore, and shared expectations" width="320" />

| Tab | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your next contribution, your next chore, shared expectations, and recent activity. |
| **Ledger** | Agreed charges, balances, and every contribution record. |
| **Chores** | Household chores, who they're for, and when they're due. |
| **Stay** | Dates, the people in the stay, house details, and outside payment options. |

Two controls sit at the top of the screen:

- **Stay name** (top left): If you're part of more than one stay, tap the name to switch between them.
- **Your initials** (top right): Opens **Settings**, where you manage your profile and reminders.

## Recording a contribution

A contribution is a shared record of something you paid or provided outside Kouchd, like a payment sent by Venmo or groceries you picked up. Kouchd does not move money. The record helps everyone stay on the same page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Ledger tab">
    Tap **Ledger**, then tap **Record a contribution** (or the **+** button at the top right).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Choose a **Type**: Outside payment, Groceries, Supplies, House work, or Other. Enter the amount in dollars and add a short note so the other person recognizes it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether it counts toward the balance">
    Leave **Count toward balance** on if this should reduce what's owed. Turn it off for things you want on record but that weren't part of the agreed amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Record">
    The record appears as **pending** with the note "Waiting for the other person to confirm."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirming or disputing a contribution

When someone else records a contribution that involves you, it shows up under **Contribution records** in the Ledger with **Confirm** and **Dispute** buttons.

<img src="/help/images/ios-ledger-screen.png" alt="Kouchd iPhone Ledger tab showing a stay contribution that is due and a pending payment record with Confirm and Dispute buttons" width="320" />

- Tap **Confirm** if it matches what you received. Confirmed contributions that count toward the balance are credited to the oldest charge that's due.
- Tap **Dispute** if something looks off. Then talk it through with the other person. If the details were wrong, the person who recorded it (or the host) can tap **Void record** and record it again correctly.

<Tip>
  Hosts with more than one guest can pick a guest at the top of the Ledger to see that guest's charges and records. Hosts can also tap **Waive late fee** on a charge that has one.
</Tip>

## Chores

Tap **Chores** to see what needs doing. Each chore shows who it's for (Shared, Host, or Guest) and its due date.

<img src="/help/images/ios-chores-screen.png" alt="Kouchd iPhone Chores tab with open and completed chores" width="320" />

- **Mark a chore done:** Tap the circle next to it. Tap again to mark it incomplete.
- **Add a chore:** Tap **Add a chore** (or **+**), enter a name and details, choose who it's for, pick a due date, and tap **Add**.
- **Occupied nights:** Use the link at the bottom of the Chores tab to see which nights were recorded.

## Your stay

The **Stay** tab collects the practical details in one place.

<img src="/help/images/ios-stay-guidelines.png" alt="Kouchd iPhone Stay tab showing stay dates, rhythm, household members, house details, and an outside payment option" width="320" />

- **Stay details:** Dates, the contribution rhythm (daily or weekly), and the agreed amount.
- **The people:** Everyone in the stay and their role.
- **House notes and Around the home:** Wi-Fi, door code, parking, trash day, and anything else your host added.
- **Pay outside Kouchd:** The payment options your host shared. Pay with one of them, then record the contribution in the Ledger.
- **More for your stay:** Your guest agreement, schedule changes, rewards, household activity, settings, and links to create or join another stay.

Hosts can tap the **⋯** button to **Invite guest** or **Edit stay name**, and open **Host controls** at the bottom of the tab.

## Joining a stay from your iPhone

- **From an invitation link:** Tap the link your host sent. The app opens, asks you to sign in if needed, and shows the invitation so you can accept it.
- **With a referral code:** On the **Stay** tab, tap **Join another stay**, enter the code, and tap **Join**. If you don't have a stay yet, choose **Join a stay** after signing in.

After you join, review your agreement. If the terms changed, Home shows **Your agreement needs review** with a **Review agreement** link.

## Reminders

Open **Settings** (your initials, top right) to turn **Email reminders** and **SMS reminders** on or off.

SMS reminders are optional. By turning them on, you agree to receive transactional text messages from Wrelik Brands LLC (operating Kouchd). Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Text STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase or any stay agreement.
