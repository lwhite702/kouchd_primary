When friends, family, or travelling guests share a home, coordinating daily life requires both practical clarity and personal respect. While apps like Splitwise focus on general expense splitting and Flatastic focuses on apartment flatmates, Kouchd was purpose-built from the ground up for **private household stays**.

Here is how Kouchd differs in philosophy, privacy, and everyday features.

<Note>
  **Zero-custody reminder:** Kouchd is a coordination and record-keeping tool. Kouchd **never** collects, holds, transfers, enforces, or processes payments. Actual money moves directly between hosts and guests through whatever payment method they choose (such as Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or bank transfer). Contribution records reflect agreed-upon records, not moved money.
</Note>

## 1. Zero custody and financial agency

General expense splitters often attempt to mediate financial transactions or push users into proprietary payment rails. 

Kouchd believes private household finances are best kept directly between the people involved:
* **No money in transit:** Kouchd does not maintain balances, debit cards, or escrow accounts.
* **Direct payments:** Hosts and guests exchange contributions directly through their preferred payment tools.
* **Shared clarity, not debt collection:** When an agreement or contribution is recorded in the **Ledger**, both host and guest see a clear, contemporaneous record of what was contributed without treating guests like debtors.

## 2. Bilateral privacy for multi-guest households

In standard group splitters like Splitwise, all group members typically see every expense, receipt, and reimbursement amount across the entire group. In shared households with multiple guests or roommates on differing schedules, this lack of privacy creates unnecessary tension.

* **Private guest records:** In Kouchd, financial agreements and contribution records are **strictly bilateral**. 
* **Guest boundaries:** Guest A only sees their own agreement, obligations, and contributions with the host. They never see Guest B's rates, contributions, or balance.
* **Shared house agreements:** Non-financial details—such as check-in times, quiet hours, pet guidelines, and Wi-Fi credentials—remain open and visible to everyone in the stay.

## 3. No daily contribution caps or ad interruptions

Generic expense apps have increasingly gated basic record-keeping behind daily entry limits (such as capping free users at 3–4 expenses per day) or showing full-screen advertisements before logging a transaction.

* **Unlimited daily contributions:** On Kouchd Free, hosts and guests can add as many contributions and shopping items as needed every day. Basic household accounting is never artificially throttled.
* **Ad-free environment:** Kouchd contains zero third-party ads, tracking networks, or sponsored placements. The interface stays calm, homey, and private.

## 4. Integrated household coordination

Flatastic provides chores and grocery lists, but lacks formal stay agreements or bilateral ledger accounting. Splitwise provides expense logs, but lacks house rules, arrival guides, and shopping lists.

Kouchd unites all four pillars of shared living in one unified space:

1. **Stay agreements:** Plainspoken agreements documenting move-in dates, agreed contribution cadences, and billable night definitions.
2. **Private Ledger:** A transparent record where hosts and guests choose **Add contribution**, record contributions, and review shared household history.
3. **House rules & notes:** Welcome kit details, appliance guidelines, trash collection days, and emergency contacts.
4. **Shared shopping list:** A collaborative pantry checklist where checked-off items can be added to the Ledger with a single tap.
