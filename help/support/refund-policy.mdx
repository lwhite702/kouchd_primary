We want you to be completely satisfied with Kouchd. This guide outlines our refund policy for the Kouchd Plus software subscription.

<Warning>
  **Household stay contributions are outside Kouchd:** Kouchd does not process, hold, collect, or transfer money between hosts and guests. The Ledger is a record of agreed contributions only. Kouchd support cannot reverse, escrow, or refund any money exchanged directly between household members.
</Warning>

## Subscription refund eligibility

- **Monthly subscriptions:** If you were charged for a monthly Plus subscription and wish to request a refund, please contact us within **14 days** of the charge date.
- **Annual subscriptions:** If you purchased or renewed an annual Plus subscription, you may request a full refund within **14 days** of the initial charge or renewal date.
- **Accidental renewals:** If your subscription renewed automatically and you intended to cancel, reach out within 14 days of the renewal charge.

## How to request a refund

To request a refund for your Kouchd Plus software subscription:

1. Submit a request through the [Kouchd contact form](https://kouchd.com/contact).
2. Select **Plans & Billing** as the topic.
3. Provide your account email address and the date of the charge.

Our team reviews requests and replies during support hours (Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Eastern Time). Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method, typically appearing in your account within 3 to 5 business days depending on your financial institution.

For any questions about your billing statement, reach out through our [contact form](https://kouchd.com/contact).
